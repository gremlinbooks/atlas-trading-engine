## What We Learned
- Runner extension opportunity exists, but global performance is not runner-only; regime sensitivity is significant.
- In top early exits, the largest give-back usually came from `RUNNER_STOP`.
- Cross-window stability matters more than single-window wins.

## Agreed Practical Direction
- Use strongest reproducible baseline as control on each window.
- Prefer small, isolated A/B changes over large multi-parameter jumps.
- Evaluate any tweak on at least two windows before adopting.
